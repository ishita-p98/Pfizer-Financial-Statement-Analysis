# 04 — Revenue: pandemic peak, contraction and stabilization

## Reported trajectory
The report's consolidated revenue analysis provides the following figures (USD billions):

| Year | Revenue | Reported year-over-year change |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 2021 | 81.288 | — |
| 2022 | 101.175 | +24.46% |
| 2023 | 59.553 | −41.14% |
| 2024 | 63.628 | +6.84% |

The principal driver is the extraordinary demand for Comirnaty and Paxlovid followed by normalization. **2022 is a poor standalone baseline** for assessing sustainable growth.

## Segment and geography
The report describes Biopharma as the core reportable segment, accounting for about **98.1% of 2024 revenue**. It also discusses Pfizer CentreOne and Pfizer Ignite activities. The 2024 U.S. revenue is reported at approximately $38.7bn, versus $24.9bn internationally; geography and product mix matter when interpreting recovery.

## Revenue recognition and earnings quality
The report describes recognition upon transfer of control, typically delivery, and certain bill-and-hold arrangements subject to ASC 606 criteria. Variable consideration includes rebates, discounts, chargebacks, allowances and returns. Revenue growth and cash collections need not move in lockstep when these estimates and payment terms change.

## Comparison with peers
The report's revenue chapter describes stronger 2024 growth at Eli Lilly, relatively steady expansion at Merck and recovery at AbbVie following Humira's loss of exclusivity. Such differences should be linked to **product life cycles**, not attributed solely to management execution.

## Analytical caution
The report's profitability tables use a different revenue series (e.g. **$61.513bn for 2024**) than the revenue chapter (**$63.628bn**). Do not splice these series into a single growth or margin calculation without reconciling scope and source.

**Report reference:** pp. 16–21, 39–41 and 50–58.
